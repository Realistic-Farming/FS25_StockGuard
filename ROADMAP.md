## Near-term (next release cycle)
- [x] 2026-10-08: **A square bale's quality follows Soil's bale condition, kept out of sight until release** (SG-3 Part 3, SOIL_BALE_CONDITION_V1; SG-3 v1.2 build brief, "Damage witness intake owned by SG-3" and "Exact SG3 / F215 condition read and notification contract"; Bob's R-15 of 2026-10-08). Soil tracks each bale's condition as it lies in the yard (RSF-F215's yard ladder). When a square baler makes a bale, StockGuard now takes Soil's birth condition into that bale's quality record once, and every daily step Soil takes afterwards lowers the bale's remaining quality by the same rule; a day StockGuard missed is closed by the next one only when nothing else about the bale moved. A bale's current grade shows only while StockGuard's record covers exactly the condition Soil holds now: while Soil is changing it, after a gap StockGuard cannot account for, or without Soil, the bale shows its recorded letter as history. Needs Soil's matching change (the operation echo, RSF-F215 v1.1). Not yet: a bale that passes through object storage keeps its record but loses its current grade, because storage opens no operation for Soil to echo (the REBIND and RETIRE joins), and Soil never resets a bale's condition, so that route is unbuilt (the unwrap policy is held, DESIGN-CHECK row 126). The player projection stays locked, so nothing is visible in game yet. The first square bale joined in a launch logs one line, for the in-game check.
- [x] 2026-10-07: **Mown grass carries a food and feed quality record into hay and silage square bales, kept out of sight until release** (SG-3 Part 2.2, the mower's grading and the square-bale carry; SG-3 v1.2 build brief :192, :204, :240, :254, :263, :267, :320; SG-2 :185, :513-517; Bob's 2.2 R-15). When a mower cuts, StockGuard now grades the cut as it grades a combine's (Part 2.1): from Soil's nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium and pH under each cut pixel against the crop's calibration, and from the growth state it was cut at. The record follows the windrow through the tedder into hay, through the baler into a square bale, and through a wrapped grass bale's fermentation into silage; grass, hay and silage are Feed-only. Foliage the mower's preparation turned into meadow has no origin of its own and stays unknown. A bale's current grade needs Soil's bale condition (Part 3), so until then a bale shows only its recorded letter, as history. The player projection stays locked, so nothing is visible in game yet. The first graded mower cut of a launch logs each grass growth-state name and the maturity class it read, once, for the in-game check of the brief's grass precedence.
- [x] 2026-10-07: **A graded field's ground save keeps only each cell's own numbers** (MAINTENANCE row 239; SG-2 :160, DESIGN-CHECK row 164; Bob's R-15). Once mown grass carries SG-3's quality record (Part 2.2), every ground cell holds its own score pair and coverage, so the ground save stopped sharing records between cells again: on a driven mown field with varying Soil it saved 227 record sets where cells could share 8. The shared set now leaves out each record's coverage and top-level numbers, and each cell keeps those few numbers beside it (a coverage equal to the cell's litres is not written at all), so every record still comes back exactly as saved. On that field the ground save falls from 261,321 to 200,140 token bytes. A save written before this loads as it always did. SG-3 Part 2.2 waits on this.
- [x] 2026-10-07: **A harvest's grain and straw carry a food and feed quality record, kept out of sight until release** (SG-3 Part 2.1, the grading core and the harvest; SG-3 v1.2 build brief; Bob's SG-3 intake Part 2, split by Desk). When a combine cuts a crop, StockGuard now records the quality of what it cut: from Soil's nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium and pH at each cut pixel against the crop's own targets, and from how ripe the crop was (harvest-ready, forage-ready, merely harvestable or withered), weighted by what each part actually yielded. The grain and the straw each carry that record, and StockGuard's ordinary carry moves it with them when they move on (a delay slot draining into the hopper is the case the bench drives), mixed by quantity, with any part of unknown origin keeping the whole stock from a score. Food and Feed are judged separately: straw is never Food, and only a food crop's own ripe grain can be. No player sees any of it yet: the assessment stays locked until release acceptance, and the raw record is never shown. A mower's grass, and anything not cut by a combine, is recorded as of unknown origin until Part 2.2 grades it; square bales follow in Part 2.2 and their condition in Part 3.
- [x] 2026-10-07: **A stock's knowledge in the player view counts only what that player is shown** (MAINTENANCE row 238; SG-1 :255, :295; SG-3 :47, :505, :507). The player view copied each stock's overall knowledge, which StockGuard derives from every record on the stock, including records a player is never shown. With SG-3's hidden quality record on every harvested stock, a trailer known in every way a player could see read as only partly known. The view now works the row's knowledge out from the records it actually shows, keeping what the stock's own amount says (an unexplained change, or a move that could not be settled); a hidden record leaves no trace. Trusted reads inside StockGuard are unchanged.
- [x] 2026-10-07: **The ground save stays compact when every ground cell carries a stored record** (MAINTENANCE row 237; SG-2 :160's shared immutable property payloads, DESIGN-CHECK row 164; Bob's intake of 2026-10-07). The ground save writes each distinct set of a cell's property records once and shares it between cells, but every stored record also carries a revision of its own stock, so once a stored property is recorded on every birth (SG-3's quality record, Part 2.1) no two cells shared a set and a mown field's ground save more than doubled. The shared set is now written without that revision, and a cell keeps its own only where it differs from the cell's revision, so every record comes back from a reload exactly as it was saved. A save written before this loads as it always did. On the 302-cell mown field the saved sets go from 302 to 2. SG-3 Part 2.1 waits on this.
- [x] 2026-10-07: **New material can be graded the moment it is born, and the player view shows what owners resolve live** (SG-3 Part 1, the SG-1 core the grading core needs; Bob's SG-3 intake U1 and U2; SG-1 :253-255, :285; SG-3 :47, :98-100, :507). A property owner can now declare that it interprets births: when a harvest (or any other birth) creates new material, that owner is asked once for each carrier the new material lands in, including a hopper that already holds earlier grain, inside the same settlement, with the cut's own evidence and the record already there. Nothing is asked when material only moves between carriers, on a REBIND or on a reload. The player view's stock rows now also show properties their owner resolves live where the material sits (with the carried record elsewhere), and a property whose owner refuses it to the player view is left out entirely rather than shown as an empty placeholder. No grade exists yet: SG-3's own producer is Part 2. In-game check pending.
- [x] 2026-10-06: **A round baler's bale takes its chamber's record when it drops** (SG2-5e-c, Part 3a, the live half; SG-2 :473, with :471's round path). While a finished round bale sits in the baler, it and the still-full chamber are one amount of material: StockGuard records the bale as the chamber's mirror and never counts it twice. When the door opens and the bale drops, the chamber's record moves to the bale in one step and stays the same record, so the dropped bale knows what went into it. If the drop goes wrong, the chamber's record ends as before. Not yet: a round bale still in the baler across a save and reload (Part 3b, after the in-game double-save check, TESTING row 478; until then such a bale drops untracked, as today), and a partial round bale (5e-d). In-game check pending.
- [x] 2026-10-06: **A harvest's cut portions carry Soil's values in a real game** (MAINTENANCE row 231, SG-3 Part 0; SG-2 :503's Soil half, DESIGN-CHECK row 90's correction). The cut witness read Soil through a global name that Soil writes into its own mod environment, so in a game every CUT_STATE_VOLUME_V1 and MOWER_STATE_VOLUME_V1 portion carried no Soil snapshot and SG-3 could grade nothing. It now reads the handle Soil publishes on the mission (g_currentMission.soilFertilityManager), as the ground-condition delivery already did.
- [x] 2026-10-06: **A square baler's bale carries its chamber's record** (the bale family, part 2b; SG-2 :473, :483, :477, :656). When a square baler finishes a bale, the chamber's tracked material and its record move into the new bale in one step, so the bale knows what went into it (it no longer starts unknown). If the bale cannot be made, the chamber's contents are recorded as lost once. A square baler's finished bales still in its bale list keep their records through a save and reload, though the game recreates them as new objects, and a chamber saved full whose bale is only made at the next load keeps its record too. A wrapped bale keeps its record through the wrapper and a save. When a wrapped grass bale finishes fermenting into silage, its record converts with it. A round baler's bale is still a later part. In-game check pending.
- [x] 2026-10-06: **World bales are tracked** (the bale family, part 2a; SG-2 :400, :894, with :473's never-a-second-carrier rule). StockGuard now treats every bale in the world as a carrier of its own, keyed by the bale's own save id, so its fill type and level are tracked and survive a save and reload, whether the bale lies on the field or rides a bale loader. When a bale is deleted by any route (fed through a bale trigger, sold, cleared by a mission, stored in a hall, or deleted by the player) its stock ends once and its record never comes back; a bale taken back out of a hall is new contents, untracked until the next load and then read fresh. A round baler's mounted bale is not tracked as a second carrier (that mirror is a later part), and nothing changes at mission end. Not yet: a baler's new bale carrying its chamber's record and the bale list's own save token (part 2b), the round mirror (part 3), and a hall's own association (the hall adapter). In-game check pending.
- [x] 2026-10-06: **A round baler is tracked like a square one** (SG2-5e-b, the round half; SG-2 :471's round path). StockGuard now frames a round baler's pickup tick too: the windrow it picks up, the litres that reach the chamber with their condition, and the overflow when the chamber fills, through the same native code as a square baler. What is different about a round baler is handled as the game does it: the full bale is mounted while the chamber stays full, so the chamber's record stays whole and the bale's birth reads it (Soil Fertilizer's half, #1100, reads it before the pad for a partial bale); no pickup runs while a bale is mounted; the unload clear after the bale drops ends the chamber's stock once; the padding a partial bale needs to animate is never counted as known material (logged once per baler); and a round baler's overflow saves and restores through 5e-a. A non-stop baler is still not framed. Not yet: the mounted bale's own record and the handover to it (the bale family, then the round mirror and its REBIND), and keeping a partial bale's forming stock pending until it drops (5e-d). In-game check pending.
- [x] 2026-10-05: **A square baler's overflow survives a save** (SG2-5e-a; SG-2 :477's overflow half). When a pickup fills the chamber, the litres that did not fit wait as the baler's overflow until its next pickup pours them in. The game never saved that overflow and dropped it at every reload, so StockGuard's record of it ended as history. StockGuard now saves the overflow with the baler (with its chamber, capacity and bale type) and puts it back after the game rebuilds the machine, so its stock reattaches with its record and the next pickup pours it in as before. Another capacity or bale type at the load restores nothing. The round baler's forming bale, partial ejection and the non-stop buffer are the rest of 5e. In-game check pending.
- [x] 2026-10-05: **A combine's in-flight grain and straw survive every save of a session, not only the first** (MAINTENANCE row 216). The game loads Combine's code afresh for each map, while StockGuard is loaded once per game, so the save paths StockGuard added to the combine were registered on the first map load only; from the second save of a session the combine's buffer save wrote nothing and logged "Path not registered", and the buffers came back unresolved. The paths now register from the game's own savegame-schema setup on every map load, as the tedder and mower save does. Bench: a second map load in one session saves the combine's slot and restores it. In-game check pending.
- [x] 2026-10-04: **A tedder's and a mower's remainder survive a save** (SG2-5bc-save; SG-2 :144 and :247, the WITHHELD save clauses of DESIGN-CHECK rows 190 and 193). Native saves neither buffer and zeroes both at load, so a save made with grass still in a tedder work area or a mower drop area lost it, and the stock StockGuard kept for it stayed history. StockGuard now writes the real remainder with the vehicle (the quantity, its type, the work-area and drop binding, the line offset; for the mower also the fresh litres still waiting for Soil's birth) and puts it back after the game builds the machine, so the stock reattaches with its record and the next pass drops what was left, the mower's fresh share still born at the deposit. Another configuration, layout or binding at the load restores nothing. Bench: three launches, each in a new process, and a second save in one game session. The ForageWagon (5f), the Baler overflow (5e) and the StrawBlower (5g) are later slices. In-game check pending.
- [x] 2026-10-03: **Plain sowing's bounded Destruction profile** (SG-2 :243 as corrected on 2026-10-03, DESIGN-CHECK row 171's NOT CARRIED clause). Plain `updateSowingArea` makes no Lua height call, so a sowing machine's own `processSowingMachineArea` call now runs inside the shovel and leveler's before and after cell sample over its work area: a tracked heap the call empties retires as Destruction, one it changes without an accounted primitive goes unknown, an unchanged heap keeps everything, and a cell direct sowing's own clear already retired is never retired twice. The fertilizing type's override is covered too. In-game observations pending (heap kept against heap removed, the nested clear, a wide seeder's read cost).
- [x] 2026-10-03: **A stock reattaches on the first load after a game launch** (MAINTENANCE row 214). The load epoch restarts at "1" in every game process, so the enumeration minted the saved stock ids again and every unchanged stock came back RESTORE_MISMATCH with a history twin of its own id, which made the next load refuse the whole save. The restore now frees those ids before it matches, and a save that already carries twins drops them and loads (each twinned stock stays UNKNOWN until it is observed again). Bench: two and three launches, each in a new process.
